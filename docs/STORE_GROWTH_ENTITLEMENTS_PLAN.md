# Store content and growth entitlements

## Implemented boundaries and behavior

- `Store` remains the tenant boundary and is owned by the existing `Seller` record. No second tenant model is introduced.
- `StorePlanVersion` is immutable after activation. Every entitlement edit must be saved as a new draft version; its database immutability trigger must cover all new columns.
- Current CMS posts/pages remain platform content at their existing routes. Store content uses nullable `storeId` ownership on the existing records and seller-scoped APIs/routes. Basic blog/page creation and publishing are available on every plan; positive quotas, scheduled publishing, and advanced SEO are separate entitlements.
- `MediaAsset` stores metadata/references, not file bytes. Store uploads use short-lived scoped object-store upload forms when configured, then server-side signature verification, image decoding/normalization, quota checks, ownership checks, and quarantine. Database triggers enforce Store-scope references and protect referenced media from reassignment/deletion. Incomplete reservations expire. With no scanner, media stays quarantined and cannot be published or publicly delivered.
- The existing affiliate ledger is marketplace-wide and already models attribution, consent, payment eligibility, reversals, audit, and manual funding-gated payout requests. Store plan access must not change the platform program or activate payouts.
- Store Buster has opt-in first-party category/product interest signals, explainable organic relevance, moderation, and aggregate metrics. Organic clicks require a short-lived one-use impression token. Sponsored placements use a separate prepaid ad-credit account and append-only ledger, provider-confirmed Stripe/PayPal top-ups, webhook idempotency, mandatory moderation, clearly labeled non-personalized inventory, one-use shown-impression click tokens, atomic per-click debits, and campaign/store/plan monthly caps. Refunds, reversals, mismatches, and late captures freeze the affected ad account for audited admin reconciliation. This ledger is not a buyer wallet, seller proceeds, or withdrawable balance.
- Store email campaign drafts, consent/suppression, and an SMTP adapter are implemented. Admins can save/test/activate supported SMTP provider credentials in Control Center; deployment SMTP remains a fallback. Marketing sending stays disabled until its unsubscribe secret, marketing gate, provider verification, plan limits, and consent checks are configured. The transactional notification outbox is a separate path and is not subject to Store marketing plan limits or consent.

## Increment sequence

1. Add additive plan-version entitlements for universal basic blog/page publishing quotas, scheduled publishing/advanced SEO, media storage, Store Buster campaign/budget limits, email list/send limits, and store affiliate tools. Existing versions receive generous compatible defaults. Basic create-and-publish remains available on every plan and cannot be switched off.
2. Extend CMS records with nullable Store ownership; add seller-scoped create/edit/publish APIs and usage enforcement under Store locks. Keep global CMS routes scoped to platform records and preserve their behavior.
3. (Foundation delivered) Add store-owned media metadata and quota reservations, direct short-lived managed-object upload URLs, completion verification, local editor autosave, and device-upload controls. Uploaded assets stay quarantined until an approved scanner approves them. A missing object-store or scanner configuration is fail-closed, not simulated. Platform logo/favicon and Store logo/favicon/banner controls use these same media records and delivery checks. Store Plan quotas apply to Store assets; platform-wide branding has a configured per-image byte cap and remains admin-only. A focused additive staff permission (`canManageStoreBranding`, default false) prevents POS access from implicitly authorizing public branding changes. Store favicon references remain in the existing theme JSON; migration `20261023000025_store_favicon_media_guard` preflights existing references and adds image-kind, tenant-scope, and complete-upload database guards with transaction advisory locking.
4. (Organic foundation delivered) Add consent-aware first-party interest signals, user personalization controls, explainable organic recommendations, Store Buster campaigns, and aggregate reporting. Sponsored delivery stays disabled until deployment/admin gates, moderation, eligibility, pricing, and budget/funding controls all permit it.
5. (Provider-dependent foundation delivered) Store email campaigns use a double-opt-in SMTP queue, consent/suppression checks, and plan limits. Transactional account/order/support mail uses the notification outbox and the same active SMTP transport without marketing consent. Provider credentials may be administered in Control Center or supplied through deployment fallback settings, and activation follows a successful connection test. Expose plan-gated store affiliate participation by reusing the current affiliate ledger where ownership and commission rules are explicit; the entitlement does not create store commissions or payouts.

## Operational gates and deferred dependencies

- Managed object upload requires a private S3-compatible bucket, short-lived presigning credentials, bucket CORS for the marketplace origin, lifecycle/backup policy, and a configured malware scanner. No bucket or paid resource is provisioned in this work. Until scanning is operational, uploaded media remains unusable in published content.
- Branding uploads crop in the browser, then the API verifies the signed upload's size/type/signature, decodes and normalizes the raster image, checks Store quota where applicable, and sets scan state to QUARANTINED. The UI never changes the saved reference after an upload failure. Approved public media remains active until a replacement is scanned and explicitly published. The browser only receives short-lived scoped upload forms; local object-store credentials are server-side. If storage or scanning is unavailable, image changes are blocked without silently substituting a device-local preview.
- Seller-branding staff access is separately granted by the Store owner. It does not follow from POS permission, and staff access remains restricted to that Store. Migration `20261023000024_store_branding_staff_permission` adds this opt-in permission without changing current memberships.
- Store Buster personalization is off until the user affirmatively consents; stored events contain product/category references only, not raw queries or seller-visible user IDs. User opt-out/deletion removes their signal rows. Reporting is aggregated.
- Sponsored placements and email delivery use explicit deployment gates defaulting off. Paid placement serving additionally requires enabled platform checkout and a matching verified provider webhook, mandatory moderation, plan entitlement, nonzero click pricing and monthly limits, a funded active ad account, and an approved eligible product. Sponsored funding is implemented for Stripe Checkout and PayPal Orders, but must be exercised in provider sandbox and a fresh isolated database before enabling the deployment gate; never use the owner database for test migrations. Email marketing remains subject to its separate SMTP, consent, unsubscribe, and plan gates.
- Store affiliate participation is an entitlement and per-Store opt-in layered on the existing marketplace affiliate system. Stores without a Store-specific participation record retain the prior platform-wide affiliate behavior. Commission eligibility and payout funding remain governed by existing rules; the plan entitlement does not imply that a Store owes money or has a payout rail.
- Organic impressions/clicks are aggregated by campaign and day. A click can be counted only once against an issued, hashed, expiring impression token. Recommendations are not AI-generated, do not use seller-visible user histories, and honor the user's personalization opt-out.

## Verification plan

Use additive migrations, Prisma validation/generation, and a fresh uniquely named loopback-only PostgreSQL 17 test database. Cover plan-version immutability, compatibility defaults, quota boundaries/concurrency, Store ownership, public/private media delivery, scanner quarantine, consent/opt-out, recommendation isolation, aggregate reporting, sponsored funding event idempotency, amount/currency/reference reconciliation, per-Store concurrent click debits and monthly caps, refunded/disputed funding freeze and admin reversal, email suppression/unsubscribe/send gates, affiliate plan gates, and existing CMS/affiliate/payment/POS/wallet/live regressions. Browser uploads use a mocked object-storage adapter. Exercise real payment adapters only with provider sandbox credentials and verified sandbox webhooks. Do not migrate the owner database or claim production readiness.
